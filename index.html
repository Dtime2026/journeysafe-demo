Stay Safe

Tips for your journey

Trust your instincts

If something feels wrong, act immediately. Move to a busier area or open the app to send a brief.

Stay visible

Keep to well-lit, busy areas of the station. Stand near help points and CCTV.

Share your journey

Let someone you trust know your travel plans, including expected arrival time.

Use the app

Send a discreet safety brief if you feel unsafe. Staff will respond without alerting anyone around you.

Find staff

Look for the orange hi-vis vest, station help points, or speak to a member of train crew.

In an emergency

Call 999 immediately for police, fire or ambulance. Always prioritise getting to safety first.
